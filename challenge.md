# 10-Week Project Challenge

Congratulations on taking the next step in your learning journey!

This **10-week Project Challenge** is designed to help you strengthen your practical skills by completing a series of hands-on projects.

- **Challenge Duration:** September 1 – November 15, 2026
- **Duration:** 10 weeks
- **Passing Score:** 70% for each project

---

## How It Works

- A new project will be released **every Friday** through the designated **Telegram channel**.
- Each project will include its own **Project Guidelines** with the requirements and tasks to complete.
- You will have **10 days** to complete each project.
- Each project is due on **Sunday of the following week**.
- Follow the submission instructions provided with each project.

## Passing Requirement & Commitment Fee

- The passing score for each project is **70%**.
- A project with a score below **70%** is considered failed.
- A **missing or late submission** is also considered a failed project.
- Each missing or failed project carries a **30,000 MMK commitment fee**.
- Commitment fees will **not** be collected weekly.
- The total commitment fee will be calculated and collected **at the end of the 10-week challenge**, based on the number of missing or failed projects.
